How to Play & Master the Garden

1
Anticipate the Rustle: Watch the foliage closely. A fraction of a second before a squirrel peeks out, the leaves shake and rustle.
2
Speed Tiers: Catching a squirrel in under 300ms earns maximum points (+300 pts) and the coveted Lightning Reflex badge.
3
Build Combo Multipliers: Each consecutive successful catch increases your streak multiplier up to 5× bonus scoring.
4
Spare the Hedgehogs: Friendly sleepy hedgehogs occasionally take naps in the bushes. Tapping them disrupts your streak and incurs a penalty!
